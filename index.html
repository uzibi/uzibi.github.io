Privacy Policy

Uzibi is a private, self-hosted home assistant used by a single family. It is not offered to the public.

Google data accessed: Google Calendar events on calendars shared with the Uzibi Google account. The data is used only to read and create family events on request.

Google data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. OAuth tokens are stored only on the family's own server.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
